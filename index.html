I'm a natural communicator based in Melbourne, with roots in Nepal. I listen first, ask the right questions, and help people see what's working, what isn't, and what to do next — whether that's a business idea, a product decision, or a tense situation in a store.

I'm a jack of all trades who goes deep: brainstorm ideas with friends and family, map risks and mitigations, research thoroughly, and observe how any business flows end to end. I'm targeting Business Analyst and Product Manager roles where communication, analysis, and practical thinking matter.
